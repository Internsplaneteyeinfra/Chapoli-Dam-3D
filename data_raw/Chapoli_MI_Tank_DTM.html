Computed inside the polygon from the DTM. Satellite radar measures the water surface where the tank held water when the data was captured, so volumes below that surface are underestimated. Use a bathymetric survey for design capacity.

Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen), derived mainly from SRTM at about 30 m resolution in this region; vertical accuracy is typically a few metres. Satellite imagery: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics.
